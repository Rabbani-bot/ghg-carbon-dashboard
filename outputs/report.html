Scope 1-3 carbon dashboard and decarbonisation scenarios

Note. Modelled case company with reproducible (seeded) activity data. Emission factors are representative values close to published DESNZ/DEFRA 2024 factors and IPCC AR5 GWPs; replace them with current official factors for real reporting.

Key results (computed, not assumed)

  • Total footprint: 14,972 tCO2e (Scope 1 1,054; Scope 2 4,423; Scope 3 cat. 1 9,495)
  • Switching the three highest-abatement suppliers (Supplier A, Supplier B, Supplier E) to lower-carbon alternatives reduces Scope 3 cat. 1 by 27.3%.
  • 12 operational options evaluated; no-regret (negative-cost) options abate 1,002 tCO2e/yr.

Method

  1. Activity data x emission factor = tCO2e (Scope 1 fuels and refrigerants, Scope 2 location-based electricity).
  2. Scope 3 category 1 uses supplier-specific spend intensities (kgCO2e/USD).
  3. Scenario: replace intensity with the alternative supplier intensity; cost premium does not change quantity bought.
  4. MACC: options sorted by annualised net cost per tonne abated; bar width = abatement.
